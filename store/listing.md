## Description

```
RitPost makes posting the same announcement to many WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups and Facebook Pages fast and organised.

Write your message once, pick groups from your saved list, and RitPost walks through them for you in WhatsApp Web. For each group it opens the chat and fills in your message, with any photos, videos or documents you attached. You press Enter, and the next group opens on its own.

HOW IT WORKS (WHATSAPP)
1. Open RitPost beside WhatsApp Web (side panel).
2. Import your groups from WhatsApp Web in one click. Only groups are imported, not individual contacts.
3. Write your message, tick the groups and press Start.
4. Send each message with Enter, or turn on Automatic mode to send after a countdown you control.

FACEBOOK GROUPS AND PAGES
1. Import the Facebook groups you've joined and the Pages you manage (kept in separate lists), or paste a link.
2. Tick where to post and choose Manual or Automatic.
3. RitPost opens each one, fills in your message and attaches your photos and videos.
4. Manual: you click Post. Automatic: RitPost posts after a review countdown, waits at least 2 minutes between posts (5 by default), stops at a daily limit and stops at once if Facebook shows a posting limit.

FEATURES
• One-click import of your groups from WhatsApp Web (groups only, no individual contacts)
• Saved group lists, e.g. "Church groups" or "Clients", for quick selection
• Attach photos, videos and documents. Your message becomes the caption
• Message templates for announcements you send often
• Formatting toolbar (bold, italic, strikethrough, monospace) with a live preview
• {group} placeholder to put each group's name in the message
• Progress panel with Skip, Pause, Stop and Retry
• Optional Automatic mode with an adjustable interval (5–600 s) and a visible countdown you can hold or cancel
• Session summary with one-click "Retry unsent"
• Post the same message to Facebook groups and the Pages you manage, manually or automatically with safe pacing
• Post to Instagram (photos, carousels, Reels and Stories) and X (posts with photos or video, and threads), with a preview and a final edit before publishing
• RitPost Studio photo editor: crop for posts and Stories, fit with a blurred background, 15 filters, adjustments, text, emoji stickers, labels and drawing
• Write a separate Facebook post with emoji, saved hashtags, the group's name and AI, or reuse your Compose message
• Free plan for everyone, premium plans with an access code: more posts a day, more groups per run, more AI rewrites
• AI writing assistant included, no OpenAI account needed: improve, make professional, shorten, translate or write versions for WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and X at once, with tone, audience, call to action, hashtags, brand voice and presets
• Emoji picker everywhere you write, saved hashtag sets and live character counters
• Schedule posts for later or on repeat (daily, weekdays or chosen days) for WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and X, with reminders, Post now, Pause and Edit
• Backup and restore of groups, lists, templates and Facebook groups
• Light and dark mode

PRIVACY
Your groups, messages and files stay in your browser. RitPost has no analytics and no tracking. It only contacts the RitPost server to check your plan's limits (post counts, never content) and for AI rewrites you ask for.

PLEASE NOTE
• Manual mode, where you press Enter for each message, is the default.
• Facebook's terms don't allow automated posting. Automatic Facebook posting is optional; even with long intervals it can lead to posts being held or your account being limited. Manual is the default.
• WhatsApp's terms don't allow automated or bulk messaging. Automatic mode is optional, and using it can lead to WhatsApp restricting your account. Only post to groups that expect your messages.
• Instagram and X limit automated activity. Automatic posting there is optional and always shows a review countdown first; Manual (you click Share/Post) is the default.
• RitPost is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Meta or X Corp. WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and X are trademarks of their respective owners.
```
