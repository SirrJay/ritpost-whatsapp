RitPost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

RitPost (“the extension”) is a Chrome extension that helps you post a message to several of your own WhatsApp groups in WhatsApp Web, to Facebook groups and Pages on facebook.com, and to your Instagram and X accounts. It also includes a photo editor that works entirely on your device, and an optional AI writing assistant that uses your own OpenAI account. This policy explains what the extension handles and what it does with it.

RitPost AI (included AI rewrites)

When you click Generate without your own OpenAI key, the text you asked it to rewrite, the options you chose and your optional brand-voice note are sent to the RitPost AI server operated by the publisher, which sends them to OpenAI to produce the rewrites and returns them to you. The server does not store your text. To enforce daily limits it keeps a usage log for 90 days: the time, a random device identifier created by RitPost, your plan and access code (if any), the number of tokens used, which platform the rewrite was for, and a one-way hash of your network address. If you enter a premium access code, the server records which devices use it. OpenAI’s terms and privacy policy apply to the request it processes.

AI writing assistant with your own key (optional)

If you add your own OpenAI API key in Settings, RitPost stores the key only in your browser. When you click Generate, the text you asked it to rewrite, the options you chose and your optional brand-voice note are sent to OpenAI (api.openai.com) to produce the rewrites; OpenAI’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that request. Nothing is sent to OpenAI unless you click Generate (or “Check & save” to verify the key), and you can remove the key at any time.

Summary

RitPost does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything the extension uses stays in your own browser on your own device.

What the extension handles

  • Group names that you import from WhatsApp Web or type in yourself.
  • Facebook group and Page names and links that you import from Facebook’s “Your groups” and “Your Pages” pages or paste in yourself.
  • Your message, message templates and saved group lists.
  • Files you attach (photos, videos or documents) for your next post. They stay on your device until you remove them, and are only placed into the WhatsApp chats, Facebook posts and Instagram or X posts you choose. Photos you edit in RitPost Studio are edited on your device; the original is kept locally so you can undo your edits.
  • Settings, such as send mode and interval, and a summary of your last session (which groups were sent or skipped).

This information is kept in Chrome’s local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. It is used only to provide the extension’s features.

What the extension does not do

  • It sends nothing to the developer except what the AI writing assistant needs when you click Generate (see “RitPost AI” below). The only third party it contacts is OpenAI, and only for those AI rewrites.
  • It uses no analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • It does not read your message history beyond what is needed to confirm that a message was sent in the chat that is currently open.
  • It runs only on https://web.whatsapp.com/, facebook.com, instagram.com and x.com, and on no other website.
  • On Facebook you publish each post yourself by clicking Post. On Instagram and X you click Share or Post, unless you turn on Automatic mode, which posts after a review countdown you can cancel. It does not read your feed, timeline, messages, friends or followers.
  • For Instagram Stories only, it asks Instagram for its phone layout in the one small window it opens for the Story, and stops as soon as that window closes.

Backups

If you click Export, the extension saves a backup file to a location you choose on your computer. That file never leaves your device unless you move it yourself.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time with Settings → Reset in the extension. Uninstalling the extension also removes all of its stored data.

Third-party services

RitPost works inside WhatsApp Web, Facebook, Instagram and X. Your use of those services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. RitPost is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Meta or X Corp.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle data from them.
