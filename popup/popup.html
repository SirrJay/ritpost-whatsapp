                  How it works

                  Write once, post everywhere. RitPost opens WhatsApp Web, Facebook, Instagram and X in this Chrome window, fills in your post for you and moves on to the next group, so you never copy and paste. You stay signed in to your own accounts; RitPost never asks for your passwords.

                  🚀 Getting started
                  1. Sign in to WhatsApp Web, Facebook, Instagram and/or X in Chrome, as you normally do.
                  2. Write your message in Compose and attach photos, videos or files.
                  3. Choose where it goes: WhatsApp groups in Compose, groups and Pages in Facebook, and your account in IG & X.
                  4. Pick Manual or Automatic and press Start, or click the clock to schedule it for later.
                  ✍️ Compose
                  • Format like WhatsApp: *bold*, _italic_, ~strike~, ```monospace``` (Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I).
                  • {group} is replaced by each group’s or Page’s name, so every post feels personal.
                  • Templates save messages you send often; Preview shows how it will look.
                  • Attach up to 10 photos, videos or documents (16 MB each). Edit photos opens RitPost Studio.
                  • Posting a link with photos? RitPost can send the text separately so the link preview shows.
                  ● WhatsApp groups
                  • Groups → Import groups from WhatsApp Web adds all your groups in one click (names must match WhatsApp exactly).
                  • Save groups you post to together as lists, then pick a whole list in Compose.
                  • RitPost opens each group and fills in your message and files. Manual: you press Enter and the next group opens. Automatic: RitPost presses Send after the countdown you choose.
                  • Pause, resume or stop at any time from the bar at the bottom. Ctrl+Enter starts sending.
                  f Facebook groups & Pages
                  • Import my Facebook groups or Pages, or paste a link. Groups are posted as you; for Pages, RitPost switches to the Page’s profile first.
                  • The Facebook post box follows your Compose message, or untick Same as Compose to write a different post with emoji, saved hashtags, + Name and ✨ AI.
                  • Manual: you click Post on each one, and the next opens after a short wait. Automatic: RitPost fills in each post with a review countdown, paces the posts with a pause between them and stops at the daily limit you set.
                  ◎ Instagram & 𝕏 X
                  • Instagram: one photo or video makes a post (a single video becomes a Reel), 2–10 make a carousel, and Stories are in beta. Choose the crop.
                  • X: up to 4 photos or 1 video. Text over 280 characters can become a numbered thread.
                  • Captions can follow Compose or be written separately for each platform, with saved hashtag sets and a live preview.
                  ✨ AI writing assistant
                  • Click ✨ AI in Compose, Facebook or IG & X to improve, shorten, lengthen, translate or fix your text, or to write it for another platform.
                  • Set the tone, audience, type of post, length, emojis, hashtags, hook and call to action. Save favourite settings as presets.
                  • All 4 platforms at once writes a WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and X version in one go.
                  • Describe your brand voice below so every rewrite sounds like you. Always check names, dates and prices before posting.
                  🎨 RitPost Studio
                  • Crop (including Instagram and Story shapes), filters, light and colour, text, stickers and drawing, all on your computer.
                  • Your original is kept, so you can reopen and change an edit at any time.
                  🕒 Scheduled posts
                  • Post once at a set time, or repeat every day, on weekdays or on the days you choose, and stop after a number of times.
                  • Each schedule keeps its own copy of the message and files. Pause, edit or post one now from the Scheduled tab.
                  • Keep Chrome open (minimised is fine) and your computer awake. If Chrome was closed, you choose whether a post runs when Chrome opens (up to 6 hours late) or is skipped.
                  ⭐ Your plan & access codes
                  • Everyone starts on the Free plan. A premium access code unlocks more posts per day, more groups per run, more platforms and more AI rewrites.
                  • Activate a code above under Your plan. Codes look like RP-ABCD-EFGH-JKLM. A code’s time starts when you activate it.
                  • The line under Start shows what’s left today. Daily limits reset at midnight UTC, and posts a stopped run didn’t make are given back.
                  💾 Backup & your data
                  • Your groups, messages and files stay in this browser. Export saves a backup file; Import restores it on any computer.
                  • RitPost only contacts the RitPost server to check your plan (post counts, never content) and for AI rewrites you ask for.
                  💡 Use it wisely
                  • Post to groups and Pages where you’re a member and your post is welcome, and follow each group’s rules.
                  • Make each post worth reading: use {group}, vary your wording with ✨ AI, and don’t repeat the same post too often.
                  • Keep a comfortable pace. Longer intervals and sensible daily limits look natural and keep your audience engaged.
                  • Start with Manual mode to check how your post looks, then switch to Automatic when you’re happy.
                  • Respect the terms of each platform and the people you reach.
