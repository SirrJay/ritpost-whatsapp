            How it works

            1. RitPost opens each selected group in WhatsApp Web.
            2. It fills in your message, ready to go.
            3. You press Enter, and the next group opens automatically.

            Manual mode: you press Enter for every message. Automatic mode: RitPost presses Send after a countdown. WhatsApp’s terms don’t allow automated messaging, so automatic mode can get your account restricted, whatever the interval. Shortcut: Ctrl+Enter starts sending.
